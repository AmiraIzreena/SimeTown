# Smart lockers, courier platform and incidents: risk register

Maintained by the township's locker operations lead. Review it whenever a
courier company, a station or a policy changes, and at least every quarter.
A control lowers a risk; it never removes it. "Residual" is the risk left
with every listed control working.

Likelihood and impact: **L** low, **M** medium, **H** high.

| # | Risk | L | I | Preventive controls | Detective controls | Recovery | Residual | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Barcode replay**: a label (or a photo of it) is scanned again after the parcel was deposited | H | M | A shipment is deposited once, ever: `courier_shipments.deposited_at` is never cleared, and the database refuses a second parcel for a shipment (`locker_parcels_one_delivery_per_shipment_idx`). A scan never opens a door by itself. | Every scan is logged (`courier_scans`); a repeat raises a `BARCODE_REPLAY` incident, high when another courier tried | Review the incident; if a courier repeats it, their company suspends them | L | Locker operations lead |
| R2 | **Barcode theft before delivery**: someone copies a label and arrives before the real courier | M | H | A signed-in courier of an approved company, at a station that company may use; the provider must know the shipment; where it tracks couriers, it must be this courier (otherwise refused and a high `SHIPMENT_IN_USE` incident). Where the provider cannot say who carries it, the delivery is recorded as medium assurance and the township can require high. | Scan log; `SHIPMENT_IN_USE` incidents; the household is told the moment the parcel is in | Staff exception to retrieve the parcel; the company investigates its courier | M where providers cannot confirm the courier; L where they can | Locker operations lead, courier company |
| R3 | **Fake tracking numbers** | M | L | A tracking number that does not fit the company's pattern is never sent anywhere; one the company has no record of is refused. Format is never treated as proof. | `UNKNOWN_SHIPMENT` incident after repeated unknown scans | Company suspends the courier if it is probing | L | Locker operations lead |
| R4 | **Courier impersonation**: someone claims to work for a courier company | M | H | Couriers cannot sign up for a company that manages its own couriers; only the company's managers (added by a township admin from the company's written nomination) or its own sign-in can create them. Manual couriers are approved one by one. | Company access history; `courier_logins` | Remove the account; review deliveries it made | L | Township admin |
| R5 | **Compromised courier account** (stolen phone or password) | M | H | 12-hour courier sessions; company and township can suspend at once (sessions end); station-scoped access; federated companies can require MFA (checked in the ID token); a login cannot open arbitrary doors (only a verified shipment's reserved door, a few times). | `SUSPICIOUS_LOGIN` incident for many places within an hour; refused scans per courier on the company's team page | Suspend, end sessions, review the courier's deliveries | M (no MFA for password logins yet) | Courier company, township admin |
| R6 | **Wrong household matched** | L | M | Delivery code, or phone with name and address, or address with name; never a name alone; never a choice between two homes; the household is told and can report a parcel (not collected by us, not ours, damaged, wrong contents). | `RECIPIENT_MISMATCH` incidents; household parcel reports | Staff exception (wrong recipient), with a second admin | L | Locker operations lead |
| R7 | **Duplicate deliveries** | M | L | One live parcel per company and tracking number; one delivered parcel per shipment, in the database | Scan log | None needed: the second is refused | L | System |
| R8 | **Concurrent allocation** (two couriers, one door, or one shipment twice) | M | M | Shipment row locked for the delivery; compartments taken with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`; one live reservation per compartment (unique index); idempotency keys on every unlock | Tests; reservation and command records | Reconciliation job releases unused reservations | L | System |
| R9 | **Hardware command replay** | L | H | Each unlock carries an authorisation signed with the station's secret, bound to the command, station, compartment, purpose and parcel, expiring after 30 seconds; the controller carries out each command once; late acknowledgements are refused; device messages are signed, fresh within 5 minutes and accepted once | `COMMAND_REJECTED` incidents | Rotate the station's device secret | L for SimeTown's side; depends on the controller enforcing the same rules | Hardware vendor, township admin |
| R10 | **Malicious insider** (staff opening doors) | L | H | Couriers cannot reopen; staff open an occupied door only through an exception request approved by a second admin (a database check refuses self-approval); maintenance unlocks only for empty compartments; everything audited | Exception list; audit log; custody chain per parcel | Investigate from the records; remove access | L–M | Township management |
| R11 | **Physical tampering** (forced door, door left open) | M | H | Hardware quality and placement (outside SimeTown); CCTV where the township has it | `DOOR_FORCED` for an open without an unlock; `DOOR_LEFT_OPEN` after 5 minutes; failed unlocks | Staff check the locker; incident record; police report if needed | M: software cannot stop a crowbar | Township security |
| R12 | **Courier company system outage** | M | M | 4-second timeouts; the township decides whether couriers may fall back on the resident's delivery code (recorded as low assurance); deposits are reported to the company later, with retries | `PROVIDER_OUTAGE` incidents; provider health panel | Wait, or take parcels to the staffed counter | L | Locker operations lead |
| R13 | **Privacy breach** | L | H | Couriers see masked recipient details only; provider recipient data is used for matching and not stored (only which details agreed); role-based access; scans kept 180 days, resolved incidents a year; secrets kept in the environment, never in the database or the browser | Audit log; access reviews | PDPA breach procedure | L | Data protection officer |
| R14 | **Uncollected parcels** blocking lockers | H | M | Reminders outside quiet hours; deadline; relocation to staffed storage with custody; return to sender; lockers are never marked empty because of a deadline | Overview dashboard; forecast; relocation tasks | Staff relocate; return to the courier | L | Locker operations lead |
| R15 | **A report nobody handles**, or one that sits too long | M | M | Every township case has one owner and deadlines set per priority by the township (`incident_policies`); critical cases go to the admins at once | The `incidents` job escalates unassigned and overdue cases to the admins, once per case and round; the dashboard counts open, unassigned and overdue cases | An admin assigns or reassigns, with a reason in the history | M: depends on the job being scheduled and on people acting | Township office manager |
| R16 | **Courier problems sent to the wrong place** (residents told to call an unknown number, or the township flooded with delivery complaints) | M | M | Courier problems go to the courier company with no township case; the courier is never guessed; support channels are shown only once an admin has checked them, otherwise residents are told to use the courier's own app | Courier referrals are recorded and listed apart from cases | The resident can still report it to the township as *Not sure* | L | Township office manager, courier company |
| R17 | **A case closed unfairly**, or a collection dispute brushed aside | L | M | The resident sees the outcome and can reopen it within the dispute window; collection disputes show the locker's records first; the history is append-only and hash-chained, and resolving never changes a parcel | A reopened case goes to the admins | An admin reviews the case and its history | L | Township admin |

## Not covered by software

- Whether a parcel is physically what the label says: SimeTown sees a door
  shut and a presence sensor, not the parcel's contents or identity.
- Theft by force, or a courier who leaves the parcel outside the locker.
- Hardware that ignores signed authorisations: the controller vendor must
  implement the checks in docs/DEPLOYMENT.md, section 6, and prove it.
