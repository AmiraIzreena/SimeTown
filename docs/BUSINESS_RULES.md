# Business rules: where each one lives

The proposal's third edition lists 32 business rules (section 14). This table shows where each is
enforced on the server and which automated test proves it. "Ledger", "API", "Concurrency", "Offers"
and "Third edition" refer to `tests/ledger.test.ts`, `tests/api.test.ts`,
`tests/concurrency.test.ts`, `tests/offers.test.ts` and `tests/third-edition.test.ts`.

| Rule | In short | Enforced in | Proved by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BR1 | Residents are verified against a unit; tenants by owner or management | `identity/service.ts` (`registerResident`, `verifyResident`, `decideTenant`); `auth/actor.ts → requireVerifiedResident`; `merchant/service.ts → createSale` | API: "registers a resident as unverified…", "lets an owner confirm their own tenant…"; Ledger: "only serves verified residents of the township" |
| BR2 | Seven roles (Guard added in the third edition), each sees only its own data and tools | `roles` on every route (`modules/*/routes.ts`), checked in `http/router.ts`; per-record checks in services (`assertTownshipAccess`, own-shop and own-sponsor filters) | API: "enforces roles on the server", "keeps one township's staff out of another township", "does not let one shop read or refund another shop's sale"; Ledger: "shows a sponsor only its own campaigns…" |
| BR3 | Shops need a business registration and admin approval | `merchant/admin.ts` (`applyAsMerchant`, `setMerchantStatus`); `createSale` refuses unapproved shops | API: "keeps a new shop from trading until an admin approves it" |
| BR4 | XP for every verified action; points only with cash or a shop's pledged offer behind them | `rewards.ts → recordAction`, `awardCampaigns`; `ledger/points.ts → issueFromPool`; `offers/service.ts → giveOfferPoints` | Ledger: "counts each verified action once…", "gives no points when the shop has not paid into its pool", "stops giving points the moment the pool runs out", "gives nothing before the campaign is funded or outside its dates" |
| BR5 | 1 point = RM0.01; no cash-out, no transfer | Points and sen share one unit in the ledger. No endpoint moves points between residents or out as cash | By construction (see [API.md](API.md): there is no such endpoint) |
| BR6 | Daily and monthly limits per person and per home | `ledger/points.ts → earnAllowance` (points); `rewards.ts → recordAction` with `activity_rules` (XP actions) | Ledger: "applies the daily limit per person and the monthly limit per home", "counts each verified action once and respects the daily limit"; API: "…within the daily limit"; Concurrency: "holds the household limit when two members earn at once" |
| BR7 | Alive with activity every 12 months; 24 months at most; reminders at 60 and 14 days | `ledger/points.ts` (`expirePoints`, `sendExpiryReminders`); job `expire-points` | Ledger: "expires points after 12 months without activity…", "keeps points alive while the resident stays active, but never beyond 24 months", "reminds the resident 60 and 14 days before, once each" |
| BR8 | Every point records who backed it and how: cash in the float, or a shop's pledge | `point_lots.funder_account_id` (cash-backed); `offer_point_lots.pledge_month` and its label (offer-backed); `issueFromPool` debits the funder's prefunded pool in the same transaction | Ledger: "gives 1 point per RM1 and charges the shop the points plus a 15% fee"; API: "shows the resident the pending request and who funded each point" |
| BR9 | Float kept apart; equals points outstanding; checked daily | Separate `TRUST_BANK` and `OPERATING_BANK` accounts; `ledger/finance.ts → runFloatCheck`; job `float-check`; database triggers | Ledger: "passes the daily float check to the sen after everything above", "pauses all point movements when the float check fails", "rejects a transaction that does not balance, in code and in the database"; `expectFloatBalanced` after most tests |
| BR10 | Unused campaign money refunded; expired value back to funder or community pool; never KITA+ income | `campaigns/service.ts → closeCampaign`; `ledger/points.ts → expireLotsForUser` | Ledger: "splits the budget, awards from the pool… and refunds the rest", "expires points… and returns the value to the funder", "sends the value to the community pool when the campaign that funded it has closed" |
| BR11 | No stored customer cash, no top-up | There is no resident cash account and no top-up endpoint. Deposits exist only for shops, campaigns and the community pool | By construction |
| BR12 | From 100 points, at most 30% of a bill, confirmed on the phone, no points on the part paid with points | `merchant/service.ts` (`assertRedeemAllowed`, `decideSale`, `completeSale`) | Ledger: "needs at least 100 points, in whole ringgit, and at most 30% of the bill", "moves nothing until the resident confirms on their own phone, and only they can", "expires a request the resident does not answer in time", "pays the accepting shop 100%… and gives no points on the part paid with points" |
| BR13 | Shops paid 100% weekly; no fee for accepting | `decideSale` credits the shop's payable in full; `ledger/finance.ts → runSettlement`; job `settle` | Ledger: "pays the accepting shop 100%, charges it nothing…", "settles every shop in full each week and shows it on the statement" |
| BR14 | Service fee charged to the funder when points are given; every fee visible | `issueFromPool` (fee from the funder's pool); `sales.fee_sen`; shop summary and statements | Ledger: "gives 1 point per RM1 and charges the shop the points plus a 15% fee" |
| BR15 | Refund: earned points taken back; spent points returned as points | `merchant/service.ts → refundSale` | Ledger: the four tests under "refunds (BR15)" |
| BR16 | Partners get grouped data above a minimum group size, unless a resident opts in | `campaigns/service.ts → campaignReport`; `jimat/service.ts → setSolarInterest`; `insights/service.ts` returns totals only | Ledger: "shows a sponsor only its own campaigns, and no numbers for a small group"; API: "…shares solar interest only on request" |
| BR17 | Sponsored content is labelled | Every campaign card and wallet entry names its funder ("Funded by …") | Visual; the label text is asserted in API: "…who funded each point" |
| BR18 | PDPA: download or delete your data; a DPO is named | `identity/service.ts` (`exportMyData`, `deleteAccount`). Naming a Data Protection Officer is an organisational step, not code | API: "lets a resident download their data and delete their account" |
| BR19 | Danger-level flood alerts and urgent safety reports override other notifications | `banjir/service.ts → recordLevel`; `reports/service.ts → createReport`; `components/shell/alert-takeover.tsx` | API: "raises a warning at 75%, a danger alert at 90%, and puts the danger alert first", "pushes an urgent safety report to staff as URGENT" |
| BR20 | Content flagged by 3+ users is hidden until reviewed | `community/service.ts` (`flagPost`, `moderatePost`) | API: "hides a post after three different residents flag it, until a moderator decides" |
| BR21 | Staff can always override an AI decision; every override is logged | `reports/service.ts → updateReport` writes `AI_OVERRIDE` to the audit log | API: "suggests type and urgency, lets staff correct it with a log entry…" |
| BR22 | No manual balance change without a second person; all logged | `ledger/finance.ts` (`requestAdjustment`, `decideAdjustment`) plus a database constraint (`decided_by <> requested_by`) | Ledger: "needs a second admin for a manual balance change" |

| BR23 | A machine drop-off counts only from a signed machine record; a guest session earns no points and can be claimed within 14 days | `kitar/machine.ts` (`assertSigned` with the machine's own HMAC key and a 5-minute freshness window, `UNIQUE (session_id, seq)` against replays, `itemVerdict`, the summary must match the items, `claimGuestSession` with `xpOnly`) | Third edition: "starts from the rotating screen code…", "refuses a device record with a wrong signature, or a replay", "gives a guest a claim code…" |
| BR24 | Location only between Start and Finish; routes deleted after 30 days; parts above the speed band do not count | `gerak/tracking.ts` (points accepted only for an ACTIVE activity, `GERAK_MODE_INFO` speed bands, poor fixes ignored, `trimNearEnds` 200 m, auto-end after 10 minutes still); `retention.ts` deletes routes after 30 days | Third edition: "ticks checkpoints by itself, ignores poor fixes and fast stretches…", "trims the stretch near home…", "refuses simulated GPS in production" |
| BR25 | Every uploaded file is checked, scanned and rebuilt as a clean copy before it is read; the original is deleted | `files/gate.ts → checkBillFile` (real signature, size and pages, EICAR test signature, active PDF content including #-escaped names, clean copy); `jimat/bills.ts` never stores the file, only its SHA-256 and the confirmed numbers | Third edition: "refuses a file by its real signature, a PDF with a script hidden by #-escapes, and the EICAR test file", "reads a bill's own text… the file is never stored" |
| BR26 | Guards record name, vehicle and host home only; no identity card; records deleted after 90 days | `tamu/service.ts` (no column for any identity document; the visitor page shows the street only); `retention.ts → deleteOldVisitorRecords` in the daily `cleanup` job | Third edition: "asks the household about a walk-in visitor… no identity card stored", "deletes visitor records after 90 days" |
| BR27 | Tamu gives no points and no XP; "Not expected" never blocks lawful access | `tamu/service.ts` never calls `recordAction`; a reply only notifies the guard | Third edition: "lets a guard scan a pass once, tells the host, and never gives XP" |
| BR28 | Danger is sent automatically only when two independent signals agree; one signal waits for a person (3 minutes for danger); every alert states the next update | `banjir/agent.ts` (`assessZone`, `evaluateZone`, `runAgentClock`, `composeMessage`); job `flood-agent` | Third edition: the four "Banjir agent (BR28)" tests |
| BR29 | Offer-backed points in a month never exceed what shops pledged | `offers/service.ts → giveOfferPoints` locks the township's month row (`offer_months`) and compares with `offer_pledges` | Offers: "gives points only up to what shops pledged this month…", "keeps each home within its monthly limit" |
| BR30 | A shop sets its own terms and monthly cap; the offer pauses at the cap; the success fee is charged only on offers used | `offers/service.ts` (`createOffer`, `offerAvailability`, `spendOnOffer` locks the pledge row; `CHECK (used_sen <= budget_sen)`; `collectDueOfferFees`) | Offers: "uses the offer on the shop's terms, charges a 10% success fee…", "pauses the offer once the shop's monthly budget is used up", "carries a success fee the shop cannot pay yet…" |
| BR31 | Sponsor benefits never include personal data; sponsored content never on safety screens; labelled | `campaigns/tiers.ts` (names and taglines only); `components/features/sponsored.tsx` is used on the home screen only, always labelled "Sponsored" | Third edition: "records the tier fee as income outside the float, allows one Title Partner…" |
| BR32 | Access needs only with consent, seen only by the resident and duty staff in an emergency, deleted on request | `akses/service.ts` (`saveAccessProfile` refuses without consent, `deleteAccessProfile`); the priority list is returned by `banjir/agent.ts → checkinSummary` only while the zone is under an alert | Third edition: "stores access needs only with consent, and deletes them on request" |

## Decisions the proposal left open

These are choices made in code. Each should be confirmed, or changed, before a pilot.

| Question | What the code does | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How does a shop's money reach the float? | A shop pays into its own **reward pool** in advance. An admin records the transfer (with a unique bank reference) once it shows in the trust account. Points and their fee are taken from that pool as they are given. | `ledger/finance.ts → recordMerchantDeposit` |
| What if a shop's pool is empty? | The sale is still recorded, but **no points are given** and the cashier is told why. | `merchant/service.ts → completeSale` |
| How is a fee that is not a whole sen rounded? | Half up to the nearest sen, per sale (20 points → 3 sen). | same |
| What if the float holds more than "points residents hold"? | It also holds money owed to shops this week and pools not yet given out. The check is float = points held + owed to shops + pools + community pool. | `ledger/finance.ts → floatPosition` |
| Is the service fee returned on a refund? | No. The points go back to the shop's pool; the fee for the original issue is kept. | `refundSale` |
| What if refunded points were already spent elsewhere? | Whatever the resident still holds is taken back. The shortfall is shown to the cashier to deduct from the cash refund. | `refundSale` |
| What if spent points are refunded after the shop was already paid for them? | The value comes from what the float still owes that shop, then from the shop's pool. If neither is enough the refund is refused until the shop tops up. | `refundSale` |
| Which points are spent first? | The batch that expires soonest. | `ledger/points.ts → consumeLots` |
| Do sponsor campaigns also pay the 15% issuance fee? | No. Sponsors pay the campaign fee (default 25%) and running costs (15%) up front, as in the proposal's example 3. | `campaigns/service.ts → fundCampaign` |
| How are SDP community programmes funded? | As a campaign with no sponsor, with the whole budget as reward pool by default. | console → Campaigns |
| Where do balance corrections come from? | The community pool, which SDP pays into. A correction is refused if the pool cannot cover it. | `decideAdjustment` |
| What happens when the float check fails? | All earning and spending pause. An SDP HQ admin resumes them under Rules. | `runFloatCheck`, `assertNotPaused` |
| When does a Lapor report earn XP? | When staff move it to "in progress" or "resolved", never on submission. | `reports/service.ts → updateReport` |
| What makes a Jimat "saver" month? | Neither electricity nor water went up, and at least one fell by 5% or more. Readings are typed in, so this earns XP only. | `jimat/service.ts → submitReading` |
| What happens to points when an account is deleted? | They are given up and the value returns to whoever funded them. | `identity/service.ts → deleteAccount` |
| Danger line at 90% | The proposal gives 75% for Warning. 90% for Danger is our own demo rule; both are editable. | `program_settings` |
| Where do offer-backed points live? | Outside the double-entry ledger, in `offer_point_lots`, because no cash stands behind them. They never touch the float, and the float check is unchanged. | `offers/service.ts` |
| Can a Shop Offer be paid partly with cash-backed points? | No. An offer is paid with offer-backed points only; cash-backed points can still cover up to 30% of the rest of the bill. | `merchant/service.ts → createSale` |
| How many live offers per shop? | One at a time, so the cashier has a single switch. Pause or end it to create another. | unique index `shop_offers_one_live_idx` |
| Does a paused offer still count towards the month's pledge? | Yes. A shop's promise for a month is fixed once made, because residents may already have earned points against it. | `offer_pledges` |
| How is a success fee collected? | From the shop's reward pool, else from what the float owes it that week, else carried and collected at the next top-up or settlement (the proposal's net weekly statement). | `collectDueOfferFees` |
| Is the success fee returned on a refund? | No, as with the issuance fee. The offer points come back as offer points and the discount returns to the shop's budget for that month. | `returnOfferPoints` |
| Do offer-backed points expire? | Yes, under the same 12/24-month rule as every point. Nothing goes back anywhere: no cash stood behind them. | `expireOfferPoints` |
| Which actions earn offer-backed points? | Only those checked by something stronger than the resident's word: a signed machine session, a tracked trip, a bill-verified saving, a garden code scan. Typed kiosk weights, typed meter readings and QR-only loops earn XP and campaign points as before. | `recordAction` `offerEligible` |
| Household limit for offer-backed points | 800 a month (RM8), the proposal's worked example. Editable under Rules. | `program_settings.offer_points_monthly_home` |
| Is the bill file stored until it is confirmed? | No. It is checked and read in memory and never stored; only its fingerprint and the numbers are kept. Stricter than "deleted after confirming". | `jimat/bills.ts` |
| What does rain or a resident's report count for in Banjir? | Each can support a Warning as a second signal; neither can declare Danger. Danger needs the drain sensor and the camera to agree. | `assessZone` |
| What if a one-signal Warning draft is not answered? | It is not sent by itself; the township admins are told once. A one-signal Danger is sent after 3 minutes only if the water is still rising. A draft whose signal has gone stale is dropped. | `runAgentClock` |
| Does an officer setting a level by hand need a second signal? | No. A person deciding is the human check the rule asks for; it is sent at once and logged as the officer's decision. | `banjir/service.ts → recordLevel` |
| Who can operate the gate? | Guards, and township staff and admins covering a shift. | `tamu/routes.ts` |
| Pass lengths | One visit 2 days, delivery 1 day, event 2 days, contractor 30 days, regular helper and carer 90 days. | `PASS_TYPE_INFO` |
| What does a carer see? | Flood alerts in the resident's zone and whether they checked in safe. Never their access needs. Either side can end the link. | `akses/service.ts` |
